## ✨ About The Project

**Life Balance Hub** is a beautiful, minimalist wellness tracker built to help you stay consistent with the small habits that create a big life. 

No more forgetting to drink water, skip workouts, or miss your medicine. Just open, log, and stay on track.

Built 100% with **Lovable AI** using a single prompt. Proof that you don't need to code to ship something meaningful. 🚀

## 🌟 Key Features

- **💧 Hydration Tracker**  
  Log how many glasses of water you drank today. Stay hydrated, stay healthy.

- **🏋️ Exercise Logger**  
  Track your workouts. Did you move today? Mark it and build the streak.

- **💊 Medicine Reminder**  
  Never miss a dose again. Mark your medicine as taken for the day.

- **📅 Daily Schedule**  
  Plan and check off your daily tasks. See your whole day at a glance.

- **📊 Daily Overview**  
  One dashboard to see everything: water, exercise, medicine, and tasks for today.

- **🎨 Beautiful UI**  
  Clean, calm, and motivating design built to make tracking a habit you enjoy.

## 🚀 How To Use

1.  Open the app
2.  Log your **Water Intake** for the day
3.  Mark if you did **Exercise**
4.  Check off **Medicine Taken**
5.  Add and complete your **Daily Schedule**
6.  Get a clear overview of your day

Consistency > Perfection



## 💡 The Idea Behind It

We often focus on big goals but forget the small daily actions. 
**Life Balance Hub** reminds us that health is built one glass of water, one workout, and one dose of medicine at a time.

The goal: Make wellness simple, visual, and consistent.
