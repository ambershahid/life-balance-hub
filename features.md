## ✨ Core Features

### 1. 🧠 **AI Wellness Assistant**
Your 24/7 companion for mental clarity and motivation.
- **Smart Chat**: Ask anything about stress, habits, productivity
- **Personalized Advice**: Gets smarter with your data and goals
- **Daily Check-ins**: Mood tracking with AI insights

### 2. 📊 **Habit & Goal Tracker**
Build consistency, not perfection.
- **Custom Habits**: Water, sleep, exercise, meditation & more
- **Streak System**: Stay motivated with visual progress
- **Goal Breakdown**: Turn big goals into daily actions
- **Weekly Reports**: See what's working for you

### 3. 💧 **Health Monitoring**
Take care of your body, effortlessly.
- **Water Intake**: Smart reminders + daily progress
- **Sleep Tracker**: Log hours and get sleep quality tips
- **Exercise Log**: Track workouts, calories, and activity
- **Nutrition Insights**: Simple meal logging

### 4. 📈 **Beautiful Dashboard**
Everything you need, at a glance.
- **Visual Analytics**: Charts for habits, mood, and health
- **Balance Score**: AI calculates your overall wellness score
- **Calendar View**: See your month in one screen
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Easy on your eyes

### 5. 🎯 **Smart Reminders**
Never miss what matters.
- **Custom Notifications**: For habits, water, meditation
- **AI Suggestions**: "You missed your walk, want a 5-min stretch?"
- **Focus Mode**: Block distractions during work sessions

### 6. 🔒 **Privacy First**
Your data is yours.
- **Local Storage**: All data stays on your device
- **No Ads**: A clean, distraction-free experience
- **Export Data**: Download your progress anytime
